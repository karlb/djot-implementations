## Caption block (^) handling. The spec defines `^ ...` only as a table
## caption (syntax.md:676), but implementations differ on what happens
## when `^` appears in other positions. Each case includes unique
## marker words (BQTEXT, CAPTEXT, FOOTTEXT) so the rendered output makes
## clear which pieces survive.

=== caption-after-table ===
| a | b |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 |
^ CAPTEXT for table

=== caption-orphan ===
^ CAPTEXT alone

=== caption-after-paragraph ===
PARATEXT body.

^ CAPTEXT after paragraph

=== caption-after-blockquote ===
> BQTEXT
^ CAPTEXT after blockquote

=== caption-eats-footnote ===
> BQTEXT
^ CAPTEXT see [^1].

[^1]: FOOTTEXT body.

=== caption-before-table ===
^ CAPTEXT before table

| a | b |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 |
